This notice describes the Xplore Horizons Inc. marketing website at xplorehorizons.com. It does not describe a client's systems, consultant monitoring, or a separate product or service.

Browsing this website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no account registration, live AI chat, or on-site application database. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to operate and protect its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab).

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is off until you choose Accept analytics. Declining does not affect navigation or enquiry links. Analytics is enabled only on the production domains, not local previews or the github.io address.

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-53C3364357) can process page views, general browser and device information, and clicks that open a configured partnership or workforce form. Google's service also receives connection metadata. We do not send form answers to Analytics.

Our integration strips query strings and page fragments from page URLs, suppresses the referrer, and disables advertising signals and ad personalization. Google's processing is described in its Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab). Provider processing may occur outside your province, state, or country.

  • Preference storage: your choice is saved in your browser's local storage as xh-analytics-consent. It remains until you change it or clear browser storage.
  • Analytics cookies: after acceptance, Google may set _ga and _ga_* cookies. Their default lifetime is up to two years, subject to browser limits and Analytics settings.
  • Changing your mind: use Cookie preferences below or in the footer. Choosing No thanks disables future analytics on this site and removes accessible first-party GA cookies. If Analytics was already loaded, the page reloads to unload it. This does not delete data already received by Google.

Partnership and workforce enquiries

When connected, enquiry buttons open an external Google Form. The website does not collect form submissions itself. If a form is not yet connected, a message explains that it is unavailable; clicking does not submit information.

Information you voluntarily submit through a connected form may be stored in Google Forms and a linked Google Sheet and used to respond to your enquiry, assess a partnership, or discuss relevant work opportunities. Google's own policies apply to its service. Check the notice on the form before submitting.

Share only the information needed to start a conversation. Do not submit passwords, access tokens, government identification, sensitive financial or health information, or confidential employer or client materials. A workforce enquiry is not an offer of employment or a promise of placement.

Questions and information requests

Use the partnership form, when available, to raise a privacy question or request access, correction, or deletion of information you shared. Connected forms identify the applicable privacy contact and retention details. Form availability is indicated alongside the enquiry links on the home page.

Client engagements and workforce relationships may have separate agreements governing access, confidentiality, data processing, and retention. Those terms are established for the relationship rather than implied by this marketing site.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the website or enquiry process changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
